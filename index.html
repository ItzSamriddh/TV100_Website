About the Event

The overarching objective of the TV100 Education Awards & Conclave 2026 is to bring together leading voices from the education sector to celebrate excellence, encourage innovation and deliberate on the future of education in India.

To be held in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, the event will provide a dynamic platform for universities, colleges, schools, technical institutions and skill-development organisations to showcase their contributions towards building a progressive, inclusive and knowledge-driven society.

With Uttarakhand as the central focus, the conclave will highlight the significant developments taking place in the state's education ecosystem, the role of educational institutions in regional development, and the opportunities to strengthen its position as an emerging centre of quality education, research, innovation and skill development.

The discussions will also explore national and global education trends, examining how institutions can adapt to changing technologies, evolving industry requirements and the aspirations of the next generation.

Key Focus Areas

1

School Education

Building Strong Foundations

School education plays a vital role in shaping the future of individuals and society. Discussions will focus on improving learning outcomes, modern teaching methodologies, foundational literacy and numeracy, digital learning, teacher development and holistic student growth. Special attention will be given to the role of schools in nurturing creativity, critical thinking, values and responsible citizenship.

2

Higher Education

Advancing Knowledge and Innovation

Universities and colleges are key drivers of intellectual growth, research and national development. The session will explore academic excellence, institutional innovation, multidisciplinary education, research and development, international collaborations, industry-academia partnerships and the implementation of the National Education Policy (NEP). It will also highlight how Uttarakhand's universities and colleges can contribute to India's growing knowledge economy.

3

Technical Education

Preparing for a Technology-Driven World

As technology transforms industries and workplaces, technical education must evolve to meet emerging demands. Discussions will focus on engineering, technology, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, innovation, entrepreneurship and practical learning. The objective will be to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry expectations while preparing students for future opportunities.

4

Skill Development

Creating an Employable and Entrepreneurial Workforce

Skill development is essential for employment generation, economic growth and self-reliance. This session will examine vocational training, industry-oriented courses, apprenticeships, entrepreneurship, reskilling and upskilling. Special emphasis will be placed on creating opportunities for youth in Uttarakhand, including those from rural, remote and underserved regions.

5

Digital Education and Emerging Technologies

Transforming Learning

Technology is reshaping the way knowledge is created, delivered and accessed. Discussions will explore the integration of artificial intelligence, smart classrooms, online learning platforms, digital resources and emerging educational technologies. The session will also address the digital divide and the need to ensure equitable access to quality education.

6

Inclusive Education and Educational Excellence

Opportunities for All

Quality education should be accessible to every learner, irrespective of geographical, social or economic background. This session will focus on inclusive learning environments, affordable education, scholarships, accessibility, gender equality and educational opportunities for students in remote and rural areas. It will highlight initiatives that are making education more accessible and impactful.

TV100 Education Awards 2026 – Recognising Excellence

The TV100 Education Awards 2026 will recognise and honour outstanding institutions, educators, academicians, education leaders and organisations that have made significant contributions to the field of education.

The awards will celebrate achievements in academic excellence, innovation, infrastructure development, research, student development, technology integration, skill enhancement and social impact.

Participation and recognition will extend across diverse segments of the education ecosystem, including:

  • Universities and Deemed Universities
  • Colleges and Higher Education Institutions
  • Schools and School Education Groups
  • Technical and Engineering Institutions
  • Management and Professional Institutions
  • Skill Development and Vocational Training Centres
  • EdTech and Educational Innovation Organisations
  • Educational Leaders, Principals, Directors and Academicians
  • Outstanding Teachers and Education Professionals

The awards aim to encourage institutions to pursue higher standards, adopt innovative practices and contribute meaningfully to the educational and socio-economic development of the country.

Esteemed Guests, Speakers and Participants

The conclave will bring together distinguished personalities from government, educational institutions, academia, industry, policymaking bodies and civil society.

University Vice-Chancellors, Directors, Principals, Deans, education administrators, policymakers, academicians, school leaders, technical experts, industry representatives and skill-development professionals will share their perspectives and experiences.

Their collective insights will help identify emerging opportunities, address challenges and develop meaningful approaches to strengthen the education ecosystem in Uttarakhand and across India.

Our Vision

TV100 Education Awards & Conclave 2026 aspires to become a meaningful platform for dialogue, recognition and collaboration within the education sector.

By bringing together educational institutions, policymakers, industry experts and thought leaders, the event seeks to encourage knowledge sharing, promote institutional excellence, inspire innovation and strengthen partnerships between education and industry.

We believe that education is not merely a means of acquiring knowledge; it is the foundation of a progressive society, a stronger economy and a brighter future.

Through this initiative, TV100 aims to celebrate those who are transforming education today and inspiring the leaders, innovators and responsible citizens of tomorrow.

Together, let us recognise excellence, encourage innovation and shape the future of education.
